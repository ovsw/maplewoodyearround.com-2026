# Maplewood Year Round

Maplewood offers summer camp and school-year programs.

## Programs and families

**Summer Camp**:
The summer programs and information for campers and their families.

**School Year**:
The programs, activities and facilities offered outside Summer Camp.

**Parent dashboard**:
The page where families find current registration links, dates and documents.

**Grade**:
The school-grade group used to organize programs and summer documents.

**Group schedule**:
A summer document that shows a group's planned activities.

**Welcome letter**:
A summer document with preparation and arrival information for a group.

**News post**:
A dated article shown in the News index and on its own post page.

## Editing

**Website**:
The public pages that visitors read.

**Studio**:
The place where editors manage Website content.

**Page Builder**:
The ordered list of sections used to compose a page.

**Section**:
One reusable content and layout unit in Page Builder.

**Draft**:
A content version that an authorized editor can preview before publication.

**Published content**:
The content available to public Website visitors.

**Presentation**:
The Studio view that displays a page preview with click-to-edit controls.
