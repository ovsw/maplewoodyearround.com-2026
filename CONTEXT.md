# Maplewood Year Round

Maplewood is a day camp and enrichment center. It runs two businesses on one
site: Summer Camp in the summer and School Year programs for the rest of the
year. This glossary is the shared map of how Maplewood is organized. Read it
before you model, query or write content.

## Seasons and sides

**Summer Camp**:
The summer business: camp groups, activities, facilities and staff for campers
from Preschool to 9th Grade.
_Avoid_: SC (in public text), camp season

**School Year**:
Everything Maplewood offers outside Summer Camp: the Play Center, preschool,
classes, parties and vacation programs.
_Avoid_: SY (in public text), year-round program

**Side**:
Whether a record belongs to Summer Camp or School Year. Most content has exactly
one side; site-wide content has none.

**Season**:
A dated period when one side operates, for example Summer 2027 or School Year
2026–27. Each season has its own start and end date. Seasons are not
back-to-back: a day can fall in no season.

## Grades, groups and programs

**Grade**:
The school grade a child enters in the fall, from Preschool to 9th Grade. Both
sides use the same list of grades.
_Avoid_: age, age group, entering grade (as separate terms)

**Program**:
Something a family signs up for, on one side. Examples: Indoor/Outdoor Play
Center, Flying Solo, 2-Day Preschool, Birthday Parties, the Preschool &
Kindergarten summer program.
_Avoid_: offering, service

**Age program**:
A Summer Camp program for a range of grades, for example Preschool &
Kindergarten or CIT (8th–9th grade).
_Avoid_: division

**Camp group**:
A Summer Camp group of campers: one or more grades plus Girls, Boys or Coed. For
example, Knights are 4th-grade boys.
_Avoid_: bunk, cabin, team

**Group schedule**:
A Camp group's printable summer schedule.

**Welcome letter**:
A Camp group's printable letter with preparation and arrival information.

## Activities, facilities and schedules

**Summer activity**:
Something campers do at Summer Camp. It is offered to one or more grades and
belongs to one activity category.
_Avoid_: camp activity, SC activity

**School Year activity**:
Something offered in one or more School Year programs: class content, a Play
Center outdoor activity, a preschool day item or a party extra.
_Avoid_: SY activity

**Activity category**:
A Summer Camp grouping of activities, for example Sports & Fitness or Swimming &
Waterfront.

**Facility**:
A place at Maplewood, on one side, for example the Lake, Laser Tag or the
Nurse's Office.

**Facility category**:
A grouping of facilities on one side, for example Health & Safety.

**Sample schedule**:
An example day for one program, as a list of time slots. A slot names an
activity or a break such as Lunch.

**Staff member**:
A person on a side's team, shown on that side's staff pages.

**Leadership**:
The staff members who run Maplewood as a whole. They have no side.

## Play Center

**Play Center**:
The Indoor/Outdoor Play Center, a School Year program. It is open on dated days
during the School Year season and closed in the summer.
_Avoid_: playground (in public text)

**Play Center day**:
One open day on the Play Center calendar, with its character, special guest,
outdoor activity and agenda.

**Character**:
A costume character who visits the Play Center on a given day, for example the
Easter Bunny. Families can also book one for a birthday party.

**Special guest**:
A visiting act or extra, for example face painting or a band. Guests appear on
Play Center days and are birthday party add-ons.

**Printable calendar**:
A PDF of one month of Play Center days.

## Families and site content

**Parent dashboard**:
The page where families find current registration links, dates and documents,
in one tab for each side.

**FAQ**:
A question and answer for one side or for the whole site. One FAQ can belong to
several FAQ categories.

**FAQ category**:
A topic of FAQs. Most categories match one page or program, for example SC CIT
or Flying Solo.

**Testimonial**:
A quote from a parent or camper, for one side.

**Job opportunity**:
A job opening for the summer, the school year or a season.

**News post**:
A dated article shown in the News index and on its own post page.
_Avoid_: blog post (in public text; "Blog" is the Studio menu name)

## Outside the Website

**Parent form**:
A parent's request to the front desk, for example an absence, an early pick-up
or a transport change. Private; it stays out of the Website and the Studio.

**Weekly survey**:
A parent's weekly Summer Camp feedback. Private; it stays out of the Website and
the Studio.

## Relationships

- A **Camp group** has one or more **Grades**. A **Grade** can have several
  **Camp groups**.
- A **Summer activity** has one or more **Grades** and one **Activity
  category**. It does not link to **Camp groups**: every group in those grades
  can do it.
- An **Age program** covers a range of **Grades**. A **Camp group**'s age
  program follows from its grades.
- A **School Year activity** belongs to one or more School Year **Programs**.
- A **Sample schedule** belongs to one **Program**.
- A **Facility** can have **Grades**. School Year facilities have none until
  Maplewood confirms them.
- A **Play Center day** falls inside a School Year **Season** and has at most
  one **Character**, one **Special guest** and one outdoor **School Year
  activity**.
- **Group schedules** and **Welcome letters** belong to one **Camp group** and
  change every summer.

## Editing

**Website**:
The public pages that visitors read.

**Studio**:
The place where editors manage Website content.

**Page Builder**:
The ordered list of sections used to compose a page.

**Section**:
One reusable content and layout unit in Page Builder.

**Draft**:
A content version that an authorized editor can preview before publication.

**Published content**:
The content available to public Website visitors.

**Presentation**:
The Studio view that displays a page preview with click-to-edit controls.
